## 4. What the ad market confirms (patterns → what to test)

- **Roster mechanic dominates** — nearly every winner personalizes **N grandkids' names** (Newsvips monsters, Canvasgiftco sign, Legend family-name, Always-with-you). This is exactly the Teeinblue Conditional-Logic + Clipart strength the Jul-10 doc's GPD01/02/04 slots are built on. **Confirmed: build the roster once, re-skin across products.**
- **Nickname breadth matters** — winners expose the full dropdown (Grandma/Mimi/Nana/Gigi/Papa/Poppy…). Family-love-gift & the Jul-10 vocab list agree.
- **Two AOV poles proven:** impulse tier $13–24 (charm, tee, ornament, sign) *and* premium $50–100 (necklace $49.95, candle warmer $49.95, canvas $99.99). Our $24 tier sits low — **the premium keepsakes (canvas, necklace, candle warmer) are the AOV-lift test.**
- **Format spread that works:** doormat · wood/metal sign · tee/sweatshirt · ornament · LED light box · necklace/charm · canvas · candle warmer. Doormat + ornament are what's scaling *right now* (Personal Chic).
- **Occasion elasticity:** the same roster art re-hooks Halloween ("boo crew / little monsters"), Christmas (snowman), and GPD — competitors are already skinning it seasonally. We can too.
