- **The candle warmer specifically = Macorner's play. PFG's ~13-creative "hardest push" is on the night-light / poster versions of Grandma's Garden, not the candle warmer.**
- The **winning ANGLE and COPY are proven across ~6+ sellers and 100s of creatives spanning 2+ years (PFG creatives date back to 2024-03)** — it's the birth-flower/roster mechanic that's the winner, and Macorner is the one who ported it onto the candle-warmer body.
- Generic "candle warmer" ads (ChillBeam Solara $89, ByMeva Retro Dome $119, AlmaGems birth-flower candle warmer) are a **separate, non-personalized category** with a totally different angle (see Appendix B) — useful for the *product/mechanism* proof, not the emotional gift angle.

**Implication for us:** we are early. Nobody except Macorner is running the winning emotional gift angle on the candle-warmer body. The template is de-risked; the format on this body is not yet saturated.

## WINNING PATTERNS (what repeats across the long-runners)

1. **PRODUCT-AS-HERO close-up, no talking head.** The winner opens on an extreme close-up of the *personalized printed surface* — you read the names/flowers in the first second. Cheap to make, infinitely re-skinnable per customer, no UGC actor needed. This beat everything for 122 days.

2. **The birth-flower roster IS the ad.** The mechanic (one named birth-month flower per grandchild) is simultaneously the hook, the personalization, the emotional payload, and the upsell ladder (more kids = more flowers = higher tier). Every seller across every format leads with the roster.

3. **Locked headline: "GRANDMA'S GARDEN — Love Grows Here."** Never reworded. It's the template's equity. Swap only the matriarch word (Grandma's / Nana's / Mimi's / Mom's) to open new audiences.

4. **Subtitled micro-story, ~15–20 sec.** Burned-in white-box captions carry a short emotional narration (e.g., "one for every child she's watched grow"). Present-tense living legacy, NOT memorial (memorial is a separate variant to test, not the proven default).

5. **"Warm glow = love at home" metaphor fuses function to feeling.** The candle-warmer's literal glow is reframed as love glowing at home / a "forever garden." The utility (melts candle from above, no flame, extends candle life) is *subtext*, not the pitch. The emotion is the pitch.

6. **NO discount, NO urgency — sell on emotion at full $49.95.** The proven winner runs clean: no % off, no countdown, no scarcity. Premium gift positioning. (Generic non-personalized candle warmers, by contrast, lean hard on "50% OFF" — see Appendix B. Personalized-gift angle does NOT need the discount.)

7. **Female 45–64, gifting Mom/Grandma, English markets (US + UK/IE).** Broad 18–65 all-gender targeting; algo finds the women. Copy speaks to the *giver* ("she's watched grow") not the wearer.
