## THE COPY FORMULA (repeats across the entire account)
Short heroes follow a rigid 2–3 line template:
```
Perfect [Occasion/Recipient] Gift For [Family/Dads/Couples/Grandma] ❤️
Order now ➡️ / Order here 👉  [cloaked short link]
[optional] Worldwide shipping!
```
- Recipient/occasion is swapped per product; heart emoji + arrow emoji are constant; one cloaked tracking link per creative.
- **Two emotional-depth tiers:** (1) high-volume seasonal/occasion heroes use the terse formula; (2) the deepest-emotion evergreen heroes (memorial, "our moon") get a 3–4 line micro-story that names a feeling ("some nights don't fade," "heartwarming memorial") before the CTA.
- **No discount/urgency in ad copy** — offer mechanics (bundles, shipping protection, priority-processing upsell) are all on the landing page. Ads sell the emotion; the page sells the deal + AOV.

## PATTERN SUMMARY
1. **Video-only, roster-personalization** on individual product pages; 5–18 dup creatives per hero URL.
2. **Concentrate + endure:** hard-push proven heroes 200–470 days across 30+ countries, US-led.
3. **Emotional roster mechanic is the moat:** multi-character (grandma/grandpa/parents/each-kid/pets) builds = high perceived value + hard to knock off. This is exactly MJ4U's Teeinblue conditional-logic/clipart strength.
4. **Memorial/grief = the single strongest, most durable angle** (least seasonal → longest runtime, fewest copycats).
5. **Data-to-product "magic" mechanics** (moon-phase from a date, crossword from names) create novelty and shareability.
6. **Terse benefit-line copy + emoji + cloaked link;** emotion carries the ad, the landing page carries the offer/AOV.
7. **Two store fronts / two FB pages, one backend** = spread risk + more ad-account surface on the same catalog.
