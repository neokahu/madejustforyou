# Grandparents Day 2026 — Crafted Design Concepts

Idea engine ([`../05-idea-crafting.md`](../05-idea-crafting.md)) run on the 4 greenlit slots.
Each concept = **Mechanic + Angle + Hook + Aesthetic → Product**, crafted from mined vocabulary,
with a differentiation move applied so it's original, not a copy of the winning listing.

**Vocabulary mined from the live pull** (the "dictionary" we recombine, not copy):
- **Grandma nicknames:** Grandma · Gigi · Nana · Mimi · Grammy · Gramma · Granny · Nonna · Oma · Abuela · MawMaw · GG (great-gran)
- **Grandpa nicknames:** Grandpa · Papa · Poppy · Papi · Grandad · Grandpop · Pop
- **Live hook phrases:** "Promoted to Grandma" · "In My Grandma Era" · "The Best Chapter Yet" · "My Favorite People Call Me Grandma" · "Grandkids Make Life Grand" · "Love them, spoil them, give them back" · "First Dad, Now Grandpa" · "Best Papa Ever" · "This Grandpa belongs to…" · "Everything tastes better when Grandpa grills it"
- **Metaphors in play:** garden/flowers · crew/tribe · era · hive · night sky/stars · birds · family tree · grilling/garage/fishing crew
- **Mechanics under the winners:** Roster ("one-per-grandchild") · Handwriting · Role-transition · Possession

## Slot 1 — GPD01 · Grandma + grandkids' names (mug) · composite 4.24
**Mechanic:** Roster (grandkids' names) + Possession · **Angle:** pride / milestone · **Aesthetic:** warm varsity + Caveat script accent

| # | Concept (hook) | Differentiation move | Visual |
|---|----------------|----------------------|--------|
| 1A | **"Grandma's Crew"** — grandkids' names laid out as a team/tour **lineup** | #2 same idea, fresher layout (roster as varsity roster, not a plain list) | Varsity block names + numbers, cream/clay |
| 1B | **"This Grandma Belongs To [names]"** | possession mechanic (from "belongs to" vocab) | Names in hearts orbiting the nickname |
| 1C | **"Promoted to Grandma · Est. [year]"** + names | role-transition, targets *new* grandmas (pregnancy reveal) | Stamp/badge "Est." + name list |

- **Personalize:** nickname (dropdown, all variants) · 1–8 grandkid names · optional Est. year
- **Lead test:** **1A "Grandma's Crew"** (broadest, evergreen). Cheapest Tier-1.

## Slot 2 — GPD02 · Grandma's Garden birth-flower (sweatshirt) · composite 4.15
**Mechanic:** Roster ("one X per grandchild") · **Angle:** sentimental pride · **Aesthetic:** warm watercolor botanical / embroidered
*This slot is where differentiation matters most — macorner owns the birth-flower **garden**. Same mechanic, new metaphors:*

| # | Concept (hook) | Metaphor | Differentiation |
|---|----------------|----------|-----------------|
| 2A | **"Grandma's Garden"** — a birth flower per grandchild | garden (known lane) | #2 our watercolor + names on stems — one entry to compete directly |
| 2B | **"Grandma's Little Birds"** — a bird per grandchild on a branch | flock | #1 same mechanic, metaphor macorner doesn't own |
| 2C | **"Grandma's Night Sky"** — a star per grandchild | constellation | #1 new metaphor; works year-round, not just spring |
| 2D | **"Grandma's Hive"** — a bee per grandchild, *"the whole hive loves you"* | hive | #1 playful lane, low competition |

- **Personalize:** nickname · N grandkids (name; + birth-month for 2A) · optional "Est." year
- **Lead test:** **head-to-head 2A vs 2B** in Tier-1 — proven metaphor vs differentiated one. This single test tells us whether to fight in the crowded garden lane or own the birds lane.

## Slot 3 — GPD15 · Grandma's Recipe / handwriting (tea towel · mug) · composite 4.06
**Mechanic:** Handwriting (her *actual* recipe) — highest emotion · **Angle:** nostalgic/heritage · **Aesthetic:** vintage recipe card, cream+ink, **Caveat script (fully on-brand)**

| # | Concept (hook) | Differentiation move | Product |
|---|----------------|----------------------|---------|
| 3A | **"Grandma's Recipe"** — her handwriting reproduced under a "Grandma's Kitchen" header | the core — handwriting mechanic | tea towel (lead), mug |
| 3B | **"[Dish]" — Grandma [Name]'s recipe** — name her signature dish | specificity / naming | tea towel, apron |
| 3C | Recipe + **photo of her** stacked (recipe one side, portrait other) | #3 stack two mechanics (handwriting + photo) | mug, tea towel |

- **Personalize:** upload photo of handwriting/recipe **or** type it · grandma's name · optional portrait
- **UX flag:** the handwriting version needs a **photo upload** of the real recipe — more friction than a name field; the typed-recipe fallback keeps conversion up.
- **Cross-sell:** this is a top **memorial/evergreen** SKU too (lost loved one) — build once, sell year-round.
- **Lead test:** **3A "Grandma's Recipe" tea towel.**

## The engine's leverage — reuse & expansion

The **Roster mechanic** ("one-per-grandchild") powers Slots 1, 2, and 4 and **ports** with almost no new design work:
- **New recipients:** → Great-Grandma (**GG**), godmother, **dog-mom's pack** (a paw per pet), teacher (a star per student). *(differentiation move #4)*
- **New occasions:** the same birds/stars/garden art re-hooks for Mother's Day, Christmas ("Grandma's Garden in winter"), new-baby ("+1 to the flock").
- **New products:** birds/stars/garden → sweatshirt → mug → tote → blanket, one artwork.

→ **Test the mechanic once (2A vs 2B), then scale the winning mechanic across recipients, occasions, and products.** That's the compounding the playbook is built for.
