# Design Brief — `GPD02` Grandma's Garden / Little Birds (head-to-head)

> Lead concept from the Grandparents Day sprint. Two variants to A/B in Tier-1: proven metaphor
> (Garden) vs differentiated metaphor (Little Birds), same Roster mechanic.

## The idea
- **Idea ID:** IDEA-GPD02 · **Composite score:** 4.15 · **Occasion:** Grandparents Day (Sep 13, 2026) — lock-by Aug 30
- **Product(s):** sweatshirt (lead, AOV) → extend to mug + tote if it wins
- **Recipient:** grandma (all nickname variants)
- **Emotional angle:** sentimental pride — "look how my family grew"
- **Mechanic:** Roster — one motif per grandchild (scales with family size)
- **Hook (2 variants to test):**
  - **2A** "Grandma's Garden" — a **birth flower** per grandchild
  - **2B** "Grandma's Little Birds" — a **bird on a branch** per grandchild

## Personalization
- **Fields:** nickname (Grandma/Gigi/Nana/Mimi/Grammy/Oma/Abuela) · number of grandchildren (1–10) · each child's **name** · (2A only) each child's **birth month** → flower
- **Defaults for mockup:** "Grandma" · 3 kids — Ava, Liam, Noah · (2A months: Mar/Jul/Nov)
- **Constraints:** name layout must stay legible at 1–10 names; motif repeats/wraps gracefully as count grows

## Creative direction
- **Copy:** headline nickname in Playfair; "a little garden/flock that keeps growing" as optional sub-line in Caveat
- **Tone:** warm, heartfelt (brand-core). Caveat only for the short warm sub-line
- **Palette:** Cream `#FBF6EE` ground · Clay `#C15F3C` · Honey `#E0A458` · Soft Rose `#D98E85` · Warm Ink `#2E2822`
- **Aesthetic:** warm **watercolor botanical / hand-embroidered** look (Pinterest-validated); names on stems (2A) or under each bird (2B)
- **Reference / differentiate-from:** competitors own the *birth-flower garden* lane — 2A must look warmer/more premium than clip-art versions; **2B is our ownable lane** (few competitors on the bird metaphor)
- **Must-avoid:** generic clip-art flowers, cramped name lists, off-brand cool tones

## Print & production
- **Method / area:** DTG/embroidered front, center chest
- **Variants:** 3 shirt colors (cream/sand/sage), S–3XL — keep test SKU count low
- **Base cost / target retail / margin:** _[fill from supplier]_ (sweatshirt = higher AOV tier)

## Test plan
- **Entry tier:** Tier-1 organic — Pinterest pin + TikTok (sentimental/handmade framing, **not** a product shot)
- **Test:** 2A vs 2B head-to-head; watch saves/comments 48–72h
- **Success → advance to:** Tier-2 $20–50 paid on the winning metaphor → product page; gate on cost-per-ATC vs sweatshirt margin
