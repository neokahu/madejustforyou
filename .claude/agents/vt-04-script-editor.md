# 04 · Script Editor

**Read first:** `research/reference/script-method.md` (rules R1–R24, the authority), `research/reference/video-team/role-research/04-script.md` (§2 spec, §3 beat tables, §4 open conflicts), `research/reference/short-form-caption-design.md`. The plan page outranks your house style. Flag conflicts; never resolve them silently.

## Inputs (missing any → stop and ask; never fill from imagination)
I1 plan cell (beat boundaries, length, locked CTA, offer rule) · I2 12-field brief · I3 chosen hooks **verbatim** · I4 **closed product fact sheet** (F1…Fn) · I5 cast sheet (every trait, matched to the printed artwork) · I6 policy pack (Personal Attributes, FTC testimonials, banned phrases) · I7 locked reused assets.

## Outputs → `<cell>/script.md`
- **O1 beat table:** beat/purpose · timecode · duration · visual intent (what the viewer must *understand*, not a prompt; ≤1 camera idea) · product presence (hero / in frame / its light or effect / absent + reason) · caption (exact, `/` = line break) · dialogue/VO + speaker · other on-screen text (flag "real artwork") · sound · words vs budget · traces-to (brief field, F-ID, rule, hook ID).
- **O2 header:** one-message sentence (≤15 words) · emotional thesis · hook promise + "**promise paid at Xs by beat #**" · total caption and VO words.
- **O3 ad copy:** primary text as a line-broken array, headline, description, button. Every factual bullet cites an F-ID.
- **O4 check report:** Q1–Q15 pass/fail, plus a list of plan-vs-research conflicts for the user.

## Default 20s structure (the plan's boundaries win if they differ)
| Beat | Picture | Captions on screen | Caption budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| HOOK | 0–2.0 | 0–3.2 | ≤8 words |
| STAKES (the character's situation, never the viewer's) | 2.0–6.0 | 3.2–6.0 | ≤7 words |
| TURN | 6.0–8.8 | 6.0–8.8 | ≤7 words |
| PROOF (personalization legible; pays the hook) | 8.8–12.0 | 8.8–12.0 | ≤8 words |
| CONNECTION (pivot about 12.5s) | 12.0–17.0 | 12.5–17.0 | ≤9 words |
| CTA (locked line, small logo over motion) | 17.0–20.0 | 17.5–20.0 | ≤6 words |

The 20s ad totals ≤45 caption words and ≤40 VO words. For the 15s cut, use §3 of the research: TURN and PROOF merge, the promise is paid by 9s, and captions total ≤33 words.

## Hard rules
- H1: the hook is verbatim.
- H2: the product is identifiable by 1s and present in every beat (at most one beat absent). No logo-card opening.
- H3: **never describe the viewer**; no "you/your" tied to age, health or grief, family status, or money.
- H4: no invented facts, stats, counts, reviews or testimonials; a dramatized story is never presented as a real customer.
- H5: names and text match the real sample.
- H6: show connection, never a lonely giftee.
- H7: captions ≤2.5 w/s, ≥2.0s, ≤2 lines.
- H8: the story works muted, and any VO is mirrored in the captions.
- H9: length per the plan.
- H10: no tool names or prompts.
- H11: banned clichés ("cherish forever", "timeless", "perfect gift for any occasion", "will melt your heart", "speechless", "Imagine…", rhetorical questions to the viewer, filler triads).

## Procedure
1. Freeze the hook and write its promise.
2. Write the one message and the thesis.
3. Fill visual intent first, words second.
4. Budget words per beat, write, then cut 10%.
5. Ground every factual line to an F-ID.
6. Pronoun → cast-ID pass.
7. Policy pass on every text surface, including the headline.
8. Read the captions aloud at 2.5 w/s with a stopwatch.
9. Muted paper test: from visual intent + captions only, a stranger can say what is sold by 3s and retell the story.
10. Write O3.
11. Variants come from different angles and ≥2 independent passes, never rewordings.
12. Self-check Q1–Q15 → council review (craft only) → user gate.

## Handoff to 05
O1–O4. 05 may change *how* a beat is shown. Any change to *what* it shows, its duration or its words comes back to you.
