# “Everyone Is Still in Your Garden” — 40-second Candle Warmer Ad

**Format:** Vertical 9:16, cinematic realism, 24fps  
**Story shape:** Suspenseful “theft” → family-secret reveal → personalized lamp payoff  
**Product reveal:** 25 seconds  
**Cast:** elderly white American grandmother, white American granddaughter, white American grandfather

**Important product detail:** The floral lampshade must show multiple family names, each paired with a flower. Example (the real product's names, used throughout): **Sophia — Cosmos**, **Donna — Aster**, **Sharon — Rose**, **Helen — Marigold**, **Sarah — Poppy**. Swap in the buyer's actual names in production.

## Production rules

- Do **not** show the warmer before Scene 6 / 0:25.
- The viewer must see at least **three individual names** printed on the lampshade.
- Make the notebook names match the lamp names exactly.
- Start in cool rain-blue tones; move to warm gold when the lamp appears.
- Composite the actual product image over AI footage in Scenes 6–8 if the AI cannot render the names accurately.
- The story’s payoff is: **Grandma may forget faces, but the personalized lamp helps her see her loved ones in her garden again.**
