# MJP-111 — Ad Copy

All written text for the ad: caption, headline, CTA, hooks, and the on-screen text in the video. Pairs with the clips in `clips/` and the final in `final/`. Angle + rules from `research/reference/ai-emotional-video-ad-playbook.md`.

- **Recipient:** Grandparents (Grandma) · **Emotion:** legacy / being remembered / warmth
- **Mechanic:** one birth-flower per grandchild, names on the lamp, warm glow
- **Reference (competitor, proven):** Macorner ad copy — *"A warm glow, a forever garden 🌸 every light feels like love growing at home."*

---

## Meta ad fields

**Primary text (caption)** — test these 3:
1. Every grandchild is a flower in her garden 🌸 This candle warmer lights up with all their names, so her whole family glows in one warm little light. Personalized just for her.
2. She raised the whole family, and now the whole family is right there with her every night. A birth flower for each grandchild, their names, one warm glow. The gift she won't stop talking about.
3. No flame, no smoke, just a soft glow that melts her favorite candle and shows off every grandkid's name. The kind of gift that makes Grandma tear up.

**Headline** — test these 3:
1. One Bloom For Every Grandchild
2. Her Whole Family, In One Warm Glow
3. Grandma's Garden — Personalized Candle Warmer

**Description (optional):** Personalized with each grandchild's name and birth flower. Flameless candle warmer + soft night light. Ships worldwide.

**CTA button:** Shop Now

**Ad name (internal):** `MJP-111_glow-hook_v1` (bump the version per variant, keep the hook label)

## Hook variants (first 3 seconds) — test 4–5
The single highest-leverage element. Rotate styles.
1. Every grandkid is a flower in her garden 🌸
2. She'll go quiet when she reads the names…
3. The gift that glows with your whole family
4. Grandma's garden — one bloom for each of them
5. A light that says "we all grew from you"

## On-screen text (the video reveal sequence)
This is the text burned into the clip in the edit — the personalization *act* is the payoff.
1. **Open (hook):** "Grandma's Garden 🌸"
2. **Names appear, one bloom each:** Emma · Liam · Ava · Noah · … (use the buyer's real names)
3. **Payoff line:** "One bloom for every grandchild"
4. **CTA card:** "Personalized for Grandma → Shop Now"
