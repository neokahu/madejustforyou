## Where the Council Agrees
- **Beat 4 asks Seedance for too much acting.** "Eyes glisten, redden, faint smile" won't render well at 720p and won't show on a phone. The Contrarian, First Principles, the Executor and Expansionist all move the payoff into his body: a long breath, shoulders dropping, a look down.
- **The B2→B4 match cut is broken.** The Contrarian found that B2 is an edit with the bed added, while B4 starts from the original `owner-v1`, so the bed disappears between the two shots. The Executor found a second break: B2 pushes in and B4 starts un-pushed. Both are real.
- **C2-e is right.** Drop `owner.mp4` because of the palm.
- **C2-a is moot.** The proof field is locked.

## Where the Council Clashes
- **Does C2-d (two rooms) matter?** First Principles and the Contrarian say it is *the* flaw. The light on his face has no visible cause, so the buying reason is only implied. The Executor and Outsider say nobody tracks rooms across hard cuts. **Ruling:** the room mismatch itself is invisible, but the missing cause is not. A blue-gold band could come from any stained glass.
- **Keep or drop the dog bed.** The Executor would drop it if the edit fights back. First Principles and Expansionist would make it the payoff, with Alex's light falling into his empty bed. **Ruling:** keep it. It is now load-bearing.
- **S-C2-H2.** Expansionist calls it the most coherent ad and the one to scale. The Outsider reads it cold as a dead husband or son, or a hospice ad. All five reviewers sided with the Outsider. Don't back H2 to scale on craft logic.

## Blind Spots the Council Caught
- **Buyer mismatch.** All 5 reviews raised this. The brief's buyer is the grieving owner buying for himself, but the CTA "only *theirs*" and the gift-store model point to a gifter, and that person is never on screen or addressed.
- **The kill metrics never see beat 4.** Hook rate is measured at 0–3s and CTR at the click. Craft money should go first to 0–3s, the beat-2 caption and the headline.
- **Believability.** Nobody has checked whether a real 6-inch panel throws a legible "Alex" (B3b), or a recognisable dog shape at all.
- The Executor's catch: `owner-v1` shows only the right hand, so the "5+5 fingers" QA fails every take.

## The Recommendation
Rebuild beat 4 around one image: **Alex's German Shepherd-shaped light lying in his empty bed, with the man looking down at it.** Cause and effect sit in one frame, the "lonely man" read softens, and the empty bed planted in beat 2 pays off. Build the light into the still and let Seedance only push in and warm the light. Rewrite the beat-2 caption so a cold viewer knows Alex is a dog. Spend the first fixes on 0–3s, not on beat 4.

## The One Thing to Do First
Build the C2-B2 still with the bed sharp and in frame, and the panel faintly glowing. Then derive the B4 still from it. Every later fix depends on that frame.
