# Review 2 — C2-reaction

**1. Strongest: E.** It names the real failure: the cause (Alex's light) and the effect (the man) never share a frame, so the concept's buying reason is only implied. Its fix uses a prop the script already planted (the empty bed). That one shot also answers C2-c and C2-d, and E swaps the micro-expression for one readable action. Caveat: A says the left arm is out of frame in `owner-v1`, so E's hand-to-bed move has to use the right hand or a reframed still.

**2. Biggest blind spot: A.** It is a good production checklist, but it never asks whether the ad sells. It waves off C2-c and C2-d as not mattering, so it would ship a technically clean "sad grandpa next to pretty glass." D comes second: it backs S-C2-H2 to scale without seeing B's point that H2 reads as a human death.

**3. What all five missed: the ad is aimed at the wrong buyer.** The persona is a grieving owner buying for himself. Meanwhile the locked CTA "Make one that's *only theirs*" and the gift-store model both assume someone buying it for another person, usually a woman shopping for a grieving parent or friend. That giver never appears or gets addressed in the ad. The primary text could bridge this inside the locks with something like "For anyone missing their dog…". As written, it doesn't.
