# Review 1 — C2-reaction

**1. Strongest: E.** It names the real failure: beat 4 shows an effect (light on a face) with no cause on screen, so the buying reason is only implied. It then fixes C2-d and C2-c with one shot that stays inside the locks (Alex's shape lands in the empty bed). It also moves the payoff from a 720p micro-expression to an action you can read, and it catches the dead panel during the 2–3s window. C is a close second: it adds the eyeline match and the B2→B4 bed continuity bug.

**2. Biggest blind spot: D.** It reaches E's bed idea but treats everything as upside. It calls C2-b a bonus and backs S-C2-H2 to scale. It never tests that ad on a cold, muted viewer, and B shows that viewer reads it as a human death or hospice ad. It also never asks whether a Nano Banana edit can put a readable German Shepherd shape in a bed without smearing it.

**3. What all five missed:** a buyer mismatch. The brief has the grieving owner buying for himself. The CTA is "Make one that's only *theirs*", and the house rule says gift ads need a giver or family on screen. Nobody asked who the cold scroller is meant to be: the griever, or the relative buying a gift for him. Nobody checked their fixes against the "differ in one place" skeleton lock either.
