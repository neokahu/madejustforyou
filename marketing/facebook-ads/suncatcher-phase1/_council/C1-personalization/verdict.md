# Council verdict: C1-personalization

## Where the Council Agrees
- **C1's concept slot doesn't carry the concept.** The Contrarian and First Principles said it outright, and the Expansionist and Outsider said it indirectly. Every personalization beat (H1, B3b, B5) is shared with C2 and C3. C1's own beats, the bowl and the hand on the wall, are grief beats. So the "Personalization" cell can't win or lose on personalization.
- **C1-b (the lonely giftee) matters.** All five agree. A man alone patting a wall is the saddest frame in the set. The fix belongs inside the slot.
- **C1-a and C1-d are fine as decided.** Nobody pushed back.
- **The beat 4 line "There was only one Alex. So there's only one of these." is the strongest copy in the build.** The Expansionist said so, and nobody disagreed.

## Where the Council Clashes
- **Physics of the projection (C1-c and B3b).** The Contrarian and Outsider say a 6" painted panel cannot throw a legible glowing name, so the ad invites "fake/AI" comments and refunds. The Executor and Expansionist say "nobody checks optics." All five peer reviews sided with the Contrarian, and so does the chair. The honesty risk is real, but only the user can settle it with a photo of the real panel in sun.
- **How to prove "not a generic dog."** The Contrarian wants a 3–4 breed flip in B2. First Principles wants an ALEX collar tag in B2 plus a photo of the living dog in B4. Review 3 noted that the flip dilutes the single-dog story. The tag-plus-photo route needs no new product artwork and also fixes C1-b and C1-c, so it wins. The breed flip goes to the user as an option.
- **The fix for C1-b.** The Expansionist wants a younger second hand (an adult child) on top of his. First Principles and the Executor want F-PHOTO. F-PHOTO adds no new cast member and no extra video risk, so it wins.

## Blind Spots the Council Caught
- **The death never reads.** The Outsider pointed out that a half-full bowl plus a leash reads as "out on a walk."
- **The 0–3s window is shared.** Review 2 noted that hook rate can't separate the concepts, and every proposed fix landed after 3.0s. C1 needs a concept-specific frame at 2.0–3.0s.
- **Landing-page match.** Reviews 1, 4 and 5 asked whether the product page and the Teeinblue preview show the same breed, name and "in the light" promise. That matters because LPV→ATC is a gate.
- **Production traps the Executor caught.** The B4 frame built from WALL-4K will get the scale wrong. The B2 relight from a gold frame will turn muddy. And two camera moves per 5s clip invite morphing.
- **Nobody checked true scale.** No advisor asked whether the shots show the panel at its real 6" size, or how much caption text the music-only cut asks a viewer to read.

## The Recommendation
Keep the script's spine. Make the concept slot do personalization work:
- **B2** opens on an engraved ALEX tag, which plants the name, gives H2 an early personalization cue and states the loss.
- **B4** puts a photo of the living Alex in the owner's hand next to the shadow, so the viewer sees the breed match, he isn't alone, and the hand no longer sits in the beam.
- Apply the Executor's prompt fixes.
- Make no claim about a projected name until the user confirms the real panel does it.

## The One Thing to Do First
Photograph the real 6" panel in direct sun and look at the wall. Whether "Alex" is legible there decides B3b, its caption and the headline for all three concepts.

## Escalate to the user
- **Does the real panel project a legible name and silhouette in sun?** If not, the plan's C1 role ("Alex's name appears on the wall") and B3b's proof framing change. That's a plan-level call.
- **What is the standard offer, and what are the shipping and delivery window and the gift options?** These fill `[STANDARD OFFER — confirm]` and are a launch blocker.
- **Does the landing page show a breed picker, a live name preview and a real in-sun photo** that match the ad? This affects the LPV→ATC gate.
- **Breed flip (the Contrarian's idea):** do other-breed panel artworks exist (Lab, Dachshund, Golden)? If they do, the user decides whether B2 should flip through them instead of the tag.
- **Can the test even read this?** The kill metric (hook rate, 0–3s) is set by the shared hook clips, and ~$20/ad is too noisy to separate concepts on CTR. The user should decide what result would count as evidence for personalization, or change the budget or metric.
- **Second-hand variant (the Expansionist's idea):** add an adult child's hand to B4 to signal "buy this for Dad." It adds a new cast member, so the user approves it or not.
