# Review 2 — C3-buyer-swap

**1. Strongest: D.** It defines what C3 actually tests: whether a second buyer exists, someone who doesn't know what to send. It then judges every frame against that. It moves "Flowers felt wrong" into beat 2, the concept slot, which is legal. It is the only response to see that F-BOX reads as the recipient unboxing, and its fix (pull out *into* the box, add a gift tag) is free. "My neighbor's dog" fixes C3-a and also C's catch that "Alex" reads as a person. Pair it with A's production fixes and B's open-box composite.

**2. Biggest blind spot: A.** Its production advice is excellent: a one-person swap edit, one continuous push, a tighter crop. But it never notices that the buying reason first appears at 12.3s, which four of five flagged as the core problem. A makes the ad buildable without making it work.

**3. What all five missed:** the ad promises a gift experience the store may not deliver. F-BOX shows a kraft box, tissue and twine. Does the POD order actually arrive like that, and can it ship straight to the recipient with a gift note? If not, the ad overpromises, and the landing page (written for owners) won't answer what a giver asks: delivery time, ship-to-recipient, gift message. That breaks LPV→ATC for exactly the buyer C3 exists to find.
