```
• his name, set inside it

6 in · stained-glass-style panel · ready to hang

Make one that's only theirs.
```
(Everything from the bullets down is the shared block in `HOOKS-shared.md` §4b.)

**Headline:** `What to send when flowers feel wrong – Personalized Dog Memorial Suncatcher`
**Description:** `Choose the breed. Add the name.` (shared)
**Button:** Shop Now

Policy (R6): the opener is a general situation ("when a friend loses their dog"), and the story is first person about a third party. Nothing asserts that the viewer has lost a dog or knows someone who has. No price or value comparison with flowers. No claim of shipping to the recipient, a gift note, gift wrap or a delivery time: the friend brings it herself, and the box and card in the film are her own wrapping.
