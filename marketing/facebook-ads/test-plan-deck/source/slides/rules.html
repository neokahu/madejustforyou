Rules that keep the results true

Setup, stops and checks

Setup

One ad per ad set, equal daily budgets, same audience and placements, launched together. Parallel rounds get their own ad sets. Advantage+ creative off. CBO only for scaling.

Stops

C0: hook rate below 18% after ~800 impressions, kill. Grids: stop as a whole at a checkpoint. B: 3× break-even cost per sale with 0 purchases, stop. Settled days only.

Checks

A concept's result is all its ads. Judge on hook rate, hold rate and CTR; sales are a guardrail only. Correct for chance when many elements are read at once.

Every ad is named with its key (e.g. MJ4U-003_C0_K01X1-H07_E0_v01) so Meta results link back to its codes automatically in Hook Lab.
