### BLOCKED for 04 (beat 5 cannot be built as written)
1. **Caption line 1 is too wide.** `Grandpa, Grandma, Emma, Michael,` measures **1,436 px** at house style (Arial Bold 84 px, E4) against a **950 px** safe width (6% sides, E57). Breaking it again gives 3 lines (E4 allows ≤2). Only 04 can change the words.
2. **E39 and E60 collide here.** At a scale where every name is ≥100 px wide (Leo ≈93 px in the 3,185 px render, so crop width ≤~900 px), the ornament fills the whole 9:16 frame and any caption band sits over printed figures. At the scale where the band clears the print (whole ornament, ~0.33×), the names are 30–80 px.
   Options for 04/owner (05 recommends **b**):
   a. Keep the pull-back and accept that the band covers the lower-row figures for the first ~1.5s (E60 breach; owner ruling needed; 07 cannot waive).
   b. **Shorter PROOF caption that does not list the names** (the print is the read), still in the top band. Frame the pull-back so the band crosses only the lower-row figures' crossed legs and snow cushions in the tight phase (no face, name or year digit), and clears the whole ornament by ~10.8s. That still needs a narrow owner reading of E60 ("printed figures" = faces and bodies above the legs).
   c. No caption 9.00–12.20 (names carry the read), and the 7-name roll call moves to the ad copy. This changes the hook-payment trace (H76 "fully paid by caption roll call").
   Until 04 rules, 06 holds B5 as: pull-back from crop width 850 px (2× SVG render) centred on Michael–Leo to the whole ornament, the band empty.
3. **H79 line 1** `She counted every face.` = 962 px + 2×10 px outline = 982 px > 950 px. Over by ~16 px each side. 04: reword, or owner allows 80 px for this card.
