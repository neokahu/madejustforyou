- **C1 · RESOLVED 2026-11-24 (user chose option a: E55 waived for this one card, not a precedent).** H17's hook caption can't meet the locked reading rules. "His bed is empty. His spot on the tree isn't." is 10 words. E12 allows a hook caption of ≤10 words, but at ≤2.5 w/s (E55) those 10 words need 4.0s on screen. E14 caps the hook window at 3.2s.
