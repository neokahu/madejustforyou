### Conflicts and questions for the user (not resolved here)

- **C1 · BLOCKING · H17's hook caption can't meet the locked reading rules.** "His bed is empty. His spot on the tree isn't." is 10 words. E12 allows a hook caption of ≤10 words, but at ≤2.5 w/s (E55) those 10 words need 4.0s on screen. E14 caps the hook window at 3.2s. Even starting at 0.0s, that gives 3.13 w/s. The locked sheet contradicts itself for any hook caption over 8 words. Options for the user:
  (a) **Waive E55 for this one hook card** (S2, which the user can waive). The script stands as written. *Recommended*: the line is two short parallel sentences and reads faster than its word count suggests.
  (b) Extend the K13 hook-caption window to 4.0s in **both** ads (this waives E14's 3.2s). 04 would then re-time the body captions: STAKES "Lily wouldn't put his collar down." gets cut and the TURN card moves to 4.0s.
  (c) Swap to the reserve **H21** (8 words). 03 flagged that it opens on a crying child in ECU (a 07 risk).
  Rewording H17 is not an option, because H1 requires the hook verbatim.
- **C2 · H17 is a flash-forward.** Its frame 1 (the ornament hung above the bed with the collar on it) is the story's end state. The body jumps back to the kids at the box with a hard cut (E30 forbids a dissolve at the hook boundary), and beat 6 returns to that exact image. Nothing in the body contradicts the hook.
- **C3 · Change to the 00A beat map (what is shown).** In 00A, the collar is hung on the branch beside the ornament and the CTA shows "ornament next to the collar on the branch". In this script Lily **lays the collar on his bed under the ornament**, because H17's frame 1 shows the collar on the bed with the ornament above. Without this change the H17 ad would contradict itself. The bed appears again only here, as the answer to the hook, and the shot is framed on the ornament and the family. 03 said not to hold on the bed "again", so the user or council should confirm this closing use is fine.
- **C4 · End-card plate (round-wide).** E9 requires identical end-card frames in all 12 ads, so beat 7 can't show this family's ornament with the collar. The end card starts at 17.5s, and that start time must match the other five scripts. The user or 06 picks the shared plate.
- **C5 · Music register.** E6 sets one music bed for all 12 ads. K13 is the only bittersweet concept on the slate. If the shared bed is upbeat, beats 2–4 will clash. This is flagged per CRITERIA §d.14 as a user question.
- **C6 · Pivot timing.** The emotional pivot is at 57–68%, slightly past R15's 55–65% (report only).
