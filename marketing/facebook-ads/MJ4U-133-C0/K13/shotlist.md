## 4. Risks and items for 04 / owner / 07 (not changed here)

- **R1 (needs owner/04, S2 waiver or ruling): beat 4 cannot meet E39 + E60 together.** The shortest printed name, "Lily", is 112 px wide in the 3185 px render, so it needs scale ≥0.89 to reach 100 px at 1080w. At that scale the figure column (hat tip → names) is ≈1,180 px tall and the year sits directly under the names; any fixed band in the 9:16 text-safe zone (y 269–1248) then covers either figures or the top of the 2026 digits. And the full "2026" (~2,400 px wide at that scale) can never be whole in frame during a name-scale pan. **Recommended:** keep names ≥100 px (E39 is HARD); let the band cross the upper ~25% of the 2026 digits during 8.0–10.8 only (E60 waiver for the year in beat 4); count the year read on B3's last ≥5 frames (whole ornament, year ≈600 px wide) and B5. The script line "then the 'Biscuit' name and 2026" becomes "settles on Biscuit and his name". Alternative: waive 100 px for "Lily" only (≈86 px at scale 0.77, which fits without crossing the year).
- **R2 (07): 1A carries two motions** (Mom's raise = primary; Lily's 5 cm lift = secondary) against rule 2 "one action per clip". It is locked by the H16 card (rule 1 exception). Budgeted 4 takes; fallback: Lily already holding the collar raised, tag swing as secondary only.
- **R3 (07): B6 is one continuous action** (hand draws back → turns into the hug). Fallback if it fails at 4 takes: start the window after the turn begins (collar already alone on the bed, Lily's arm leaving frame), still inside the script's "hand withdrawing".
- **R4 (owner FYI): "BISCUIT" on the tag only reads where the tag face is ≥70 px:** 1Ba, B6 and likely B2. In 1A (MS) the tag is ≈30–40 px and reads as texture. The caption names Biscuit there, so the meaning holds.
- **R5 (07 cold read): H17 → B2 at 2.0s.** The hook caption is still up while the ornament is back in Mom's hand. The half-decorated tree and the open box at 2.00 mark "earlier"; 07 should confirm there is no visual contradiction (rule 8).
- **R6 (07): live wardrobe without Santa hats.** The print has hats; the live cast doesn't wear them (hats would read costume-like in a memorial). Every other trait matches the print (refs/REFS-PLAN.md match sheet).
- **R7: the collar is sized for a large dog** (Golden Retriever), coiled to fit a child's two hands; the script's "small red dog collar" is read as "small in her hands".
- **R8: no live dog.** Every brief states the bed is empty and the room has no animals; any take with a dog, dog hair shape or second pet is rejected outright.
