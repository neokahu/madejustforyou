## Locked (copied from BRIEF.md; identical on every card)

## Product truth used by every card
- The ornament is shown **only** as real product artwork: the hero image, or a preview exported from the store's real personalizer for the cast of that card.
  Names and year are never drawn by a generator; the ornament is composited into scenes (BRIEF rule).
- Hero artwork (`hero-family-2026-tree.png`), read from the image: **Bailey** (white short-hair dog, open-mouth smile) · **Mary** (kid girl, brown bob, red Fair Isle sweater, purple trousers, mug) ·
  **Jacob** (man, brown skin, bald top, grey-brown beard, round glasses, dark Fair Isle sweater, red trousers, whipped-cream drink) · **Kobe** (kid boy, curly hair, green sweater, plaid pyjama trousers, mug) ·
  **Susan** (kid girl, long locs, red plaid pyjamas, whipped-cream drink) · **Luna** (long-hair cat, white with dark brown/black patches) · all in Santa hats on a snowy ledge · year **2026** in wooden numbers with snowmen, trees, stars.
  No adult woman is on the hero artwork, so any card that casts a mother/wife needs a new personalizer render.
- Character types available (F5): man, woman, kid boy, kid girl, baby, dog, cat. There is no "older adult" type: grandparents are built as man/woman with grey hair + glasses (F6).
  **To verify in the personalizer before 05 locks references:** grey/white is an available hair color.
