- **The sixth slot, K01 or K07:** K01 (83) is the higher median. It gives the slate six different structures (ST1–ST7 except ST4) and it is the natural control.
  K07 (78) would add AN8 and the creator-style format (PX2). But AN8 is graded WEAK, two judges flagged an IC4 endorsement risk, it needs lip-sync
  (production risk median 2), and it repeats K02's comic register (ST7·EM4). Recommendation: K01. K07 is the Gate 0 alternative if the user wants PX2 tested this season

- **Not chosen:** K09 (74; judge C kill: an outer-circle giver can't plausibly configure a whole-family ornament; judge A: the hook line reads creepy).
- **Reserves:** a reserve must be a different card, and the twin constraints leave only four unused survivors (K05, K03, K07, K09).
